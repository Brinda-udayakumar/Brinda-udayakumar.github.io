Autonomous drone systems research

Research spanning perception and decision making for autonomous drones. I train and evaluate YOLO11‑OBB detectors for small aerial targets on RGB and infrared imagery across an NVIDIA A100 cluster, and wrote a custom rotated IoU evaluation pipeline because standard IoU misreports oriented boxes. On the decision side I designed a real‑time task allocation algorithm for a defending fleet, backed by a Python simulation framework that measures failure across thousands of randomized engagements. A third thread covers navigation without GPS using stereo visual inertial odometry integrated into PX4.

NLP & LLMs Arizona State University Feb – Apr 2026

NanoChat disease detection system

A conversational system that reads how people actually describe being unwell and maps it onto 721 disease classes. The hard part was never the classifier. Free text like "my lower back has been burning since Tuesday" has to become a structured symptom vector, so I built a hybrid extraction pipeline with synonym matching and contextual body‑part inference and fed it into a 561M parameter transformer LLM. Top‑5 accuracy above 97% matters more than top‑1 here, because the output is a shortlist for a clinician, not a diagnosis.

Medical AI Arizona State University Sep – Nov 2025

Unified chest X‑ray analysis pipeline

One pipeline that classifies, segments and localizes lesions on chest radiographs, using a Swin Transformer backbone for classification, Swin‑UNet for segmentation and Mask R‑CNN for lesion localization. I ran it end to end across three public datasets with genuinely different labeling conventions, ChestX‑ray14, SIIM‑ACR and VinDr‑CXR, and reached performance comparable to published baselines. Preprocessing, augmentation, training and prediction visualization are all part of the same reproducible path.
